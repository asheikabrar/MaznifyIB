    What the labels mean
    • Not studied You haven't added it to revision yet — tick the box and submit at the top to seed it.
    • Learning A new topic in the early ramp-up — short intervals as the algorithm calibrates.
    • In rotation Settled into long-term revision — intervals grow each time you rate Good or Easy.
    • Relearning You rated Again recently — pulled back to short intervals until you're solid again.

    💡 How spaced revision works

    Tick topics you've already studied, choose how well you remember each, and click Add selected. Each topic is then scheduled by FSRS — it shows up in Today on the right day automatically.

    Each review you rate Again / Hard / Good / Easy: Easy pushes the next review weeks out, Again pulls it back to short intervals.

    Need to take a topic back out of revision? Click the ↺ Reset button on its row — it goes back to "Not studied" without losing review history.
